TrainLedger

A fast, simple workout log for iPhone. Log a set in a tap, see your rest timer and next set on the Lock Screen and Apple Watch, and track your personal records.

  • Log sets from the app, the Lock Screen or your Apple Watch.
  • Personal records, volume and trends for every exercise.
  • Bodyweight and weighted bodyweight sets (BW + 25).
  • Daily backups to iCloud Drive, and CSV export.
  • Optional Workout File, so assistants you connect (like Claude with Google Drive) can see your latest workout.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your workouts stay on your iPhone.
