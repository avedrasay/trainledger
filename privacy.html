TrainLedger

Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

TrainLedger is a workout log for iPhone. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your workouts stay on your iPhone and in places you choose. The developer never receives them.

What TrainLedger stores

Everything you enter: exercises, sets, weights and reps, workout labels and notes, presets, your bodyweight, and your settings. It's stored on your iPhone, inside the app.

Where your data can go

Only to the places below, and only the ones you use:

  • Automatic backups. Once a day, TrainLedger saves a backup file to your iCloud Drive, or on your iPhone if iCloud Drive isn't available. iCloud is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Exports. When you export a backup or a CSV, the file goes where you send it.
  • Workout File (off unless you turn it on). TrainLedger keeps one file, “TrainLedger Latest Workout.json”, up to date in a folder you choose. It holds your latest workout and a summary of the last 7 days. Apps and assistants you give access to that folder can read it.
  • Google Drive (only if you connect it, see below).

Reminders, the rest timer, Live Activities on the Lock Screen and the Apple Watch tile all run on your devices. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any analytics or advertising service.

Google Drive

If you tap Connect Google Drive in Settings › Workout File, you sign in with Google on Google's own page. TrainLedger asks only for the drive.file permission. That permission lets TrainLedger see and change only the files it created itself. It cannot see any of your other files in Google Drive.

  • What TrainLedger sends: the Workout File (your latest workout and the last 7 days). It creates the file once and then keeps updating that same file.
  • What TrainLedger reads from Google: only what it needs to update that file, such as the file's ID.
  • Your sign-in: Google's sign-in token is kept in your iPhone's Keychain. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • Disconnecting: turn it off in Settings › Workout File, or remove TrainLedger at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The file stays in your Drive until you delete it.

TrainLedger's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. TrainLedger doesn't use this information for ads, doesn't sell it, and doesn't share it with anyone. No person reads it.

Apps and assistants you connect

If you let another app or assistant read your Workout File (for example, Claude with Google Drive), that service handles the file under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting TrainLedger deletes the data on your iPhone. Backups in iCloud Drive and a Workout File in Google Drive or another folder stay until you delete them there. The developer holds no copy, so there's nothing for us to delete.

Children

TrainLedger doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
